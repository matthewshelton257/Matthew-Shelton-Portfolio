# Scoring rubric · version 1.0

Score each unedited output before rewriting. Cite an exact passage for every deduction. Use 0, 1 or 2 for each dimension, maximum 12. Do not convert this small ordinal score into a scientific accuracy percentage.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Evidence fidelity | Invented capability, quote or performance claim | Ambiguous claim or incomplete source mapping | Product claims supported and accurately mapped to F1–F7 |
| Behavioral logic | Incorrect audience or event logic | Mostly coherent with an incomplete condition | All three audiences and rechecks match the brief |
| Eligibility controls | Missing eligibility or opt-out suppression | Present but incomplete | Eligibility plus unsubscribed/closed-account suppression and action recheck |
| Campaign usefulness | Generic or conflicting actions | Usable after meaningful edits | Specific, supportive copy with one relevant CTA per email |
| Output adherence | Multiple missing required sections | Minor omission or copy constraint violation | All requested sections and constraints satisfied |
| Experiment quality | Invented results or invalid success claim | Test proposed with missing measurement detail | Randomization, primary outcome, window, guardrails and baseline caveat |

## Critical failures

Flag separately from the total:
- Invented product capability, customer quote or measured result.
- Missing marketing eligibility or unsubscribe suppression.
- Proposal to send to a user who already completed the target action.
- Presenting unverified destinations as ready to use.

A high total does not cancel a critical failure. Any critical failure requires revision before a reviewer could approve the campaign.

## Comparisons

Show all run scores and failure counts per tool. Report median and range only as descriptive summaries. With three runs per tool, do not claim statistical significance. If settings or underlying models differ or are undisclosed, compare the observed tool experiences and state that confound.
