# Consumer growth & international GTM

Professional experience summaries. These describe reported contributions, not isolated experiment results.

## Heelys · Consumer growth


### Connecting customer insight to commercial execution

At BBC International, Matthew led integrated Heelys growth initiatives combining customer insights, campaign strategy, creative optimization and commercial execution. The work contributed to 100%+ sales growth and 75%+ sell-through improvement.

His broader role connects acquisition, engagement, conversion and retention across ecommerce, digital and retail channels. It includes lifecycle strategy informed by segmentation and behavioral insights, performance analysis and collaboration with ecommerce, creative, sales, product and operations teams.

The growth perspective: the campaign promise, digital experience and path to purchase need to work together. These results reflect contributing initiatives rather than an isolated experiment or sole-person attribution.

## DVS · International go-to-market

### One relaunch, 12 markets, three product seasons

Matthew led the international DVS relaunch across 12 distributor markets and three product seasons. Localized GTM strategy, lifecycle programs and partner marketing contributed to 150%+ brand-awareness growth.

This experience brings a practical perspective on scaling: connect a shared brand direction with the needs of individual markets, partners and customer journeys. The public summary does not claim market-by-market outcomes or disclose internal campaign data.

## Andrews University · Community and audience growth
