## Heelys · Consumer growth


### Connecting customer insight to commercial execution

At BBC International, Matthew led integrated Heelys growth initiatives combining customer insights, campaign strategy, creative optimization and commercial execution. The work contributed to 100%+ sales growth and 75%+ sell-through improvement.

His broader role connects acquisition, engagement, conversion and retention across ecommerce, digital and retail channels. The growth perspective: the campaign promise, digital experience and path to purchase need to work together. These results reflect contributing initiatives rather than an isolated experiment or sole-person attribution.
