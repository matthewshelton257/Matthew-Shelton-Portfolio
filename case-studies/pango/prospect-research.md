## How the shortlist would be used

Start with a coherent footwear cohort. Thursday Boot Company, Taos and Nisolo were proposed for initial fit checks. Nisolo's named returns provider creates an incumbent-fit question; Rothy's was a reserve candidate given its established return network. Apparel remains a separate possible cohort.

The buyer hypothesis is an ecommerce or operations leader, with customer experience leadership as a possible champion. No named buyer, verified business email or purchase timeline is represented here.

## Tracker created

The Notion database includes company, category, research order, source, observation, buyer-role hypothesis, account clearance, contact, business email, message version, first-send date, next action, next-action date, stage and outcome.

**Pipeline stages:** Research → Ready → Contacted → Replied → Qualified → Demo booked → Demo held → Closed.

Research, pipeline and follow-up views were created to support day-to-day execution. The next action on every current record is founder clearance and product-fit confirmation, followed by buyer verification. The database is built; the sales pipeline is not active.
