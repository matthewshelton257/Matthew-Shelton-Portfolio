# Pango · Delivery plan and measurement

Prepared execution asset. No prospect campaign has launched and no results are claimed. AI-assisted project material; copy edited for portfolio formatting.

## Four-week execution plan

Week numbers are relative to an agreed kickoff, not calendar commitments.

| Week | Matthew’s work | Pango input | Exit condition | Time budget |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 : Confirm fit | Validate ten accounts; choose one segment and buyer; revise copy; define metrics | Intake, exclusions, product claims, sending access | Cohort rules and first ten approved | 5 hours |
| 2 : First wave | Identify verified role holders; send to ten cleared accounts; record delivery and replies | Answer product questions and accept handoffs | Delivery reviewed and first objections captured | 5 hours |
| 3 : Extend carefully | Follow up; revise one element if needed; expand to 20–30 additional accounts if feasible | Confirm expansion and any message changes | 30–40 total accounts attempted or documented reason to stop | 5 hours |
| 4 : Finish and decide | Complete eligible follow-ups; reconcile outcomes; prepare final memo and handoff | Review recommendation and next step | Evidence-backed continue / revise / stop decision | 5 hours |

Thirty to forty is a scope ceiling, not a quota. If account/contact verification exceeds the time budget, reduce cohort size rather than lowering research quality.

## How to use the prospect database

One row represents one company. Keep one active contact per account in this pilot.

- Research → Ready only after the launch checks pass.
- Ready → Contacted after an actual send; record the date and message version.
- Contacted → Replied for a human response; classify the response in the page log.
- Replied → Qualified only after the qualification rule below is met.
- Qualified → Demo booked → Demo held as those events occur.
- Closed for not interested, no fit or completed no-response sequence.
Use **Next action** and **Next action date** as the daily queue. Leave event dates and contact fields blank until known.

## Metric definitions

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Attempted accounts | Unique accounts receiving an initial send |
| Delivered accounts | Attempted accounts minus confirmed failed delivery; delivery is not proof of inbox placement |
| Human replies | Accounts with a nonautomated reply; count once |
| Positive replies | Accounts expressing relevant interest; exclude out-of-office and simple referrals |
| Qualified conversations | Unique accounts with fit confirmed, a relevant owner/champion, an expressed need and agreement to a substantive next step |
| Demos booked / held | Separate counts; a booking is not a completed meeting |
| Positive reply rate | Positive-reply accounts ÷ delivered accounts |
| Qualified conversation rate | Qualified accounts ÷ delivered accounts |
| Demo attendance | Demos held ÷ demos booked; report pending meetings separately |

Use **N/A** when the denominator is zero. Do not present empty cells as measured zero. No opens-based performance claims; opens are not a reliable measure of buyer intent.

## Experiment design and decision rules

This is an exploratory pilot; ten accounts cannot establish a reliable winner. Start with one message and collect qualitative feedback. If Pango approves a 30–40 account cohort, two balanced groups may explore coordination versus exchange-journey framing. Assign at account level and keep buyer role, channel, timing and follow-up comparable. Report raw counts and confounders; do not claim statistical significance.

**After the first ten:** inspect failed delivery, contact relevance and objections. If no meaningful replies, check data and targeting before rewriting.
**Expand:** proposed signal is three qualified conversations by the end of the full cohort, plus a repeatable problem pattern and feasible product fit.
**Revise:** interest without qualification suggests a buyer/segment mismatch; relevant objections may suggest a message or offer issue.
**Stop:** unsupported product fit, founder withdrawal, repeated delivery problems or a fully completed sequence with no useful signal. Do not keep adding volume to hide weak fit.

These are proposed operating decisions to agree with Steve, not benchmarks or promised results. A seasonal or procurement timing issue may justify postponing a cohort.
