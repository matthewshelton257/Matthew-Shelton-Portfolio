# Pango · Strategy and founder intake

Independent project artifact prepared with AI assistance. The plan below describes a proposed engagement, not completed execution. Research snapshot: October 29, 2026. Copy edited for portfolio formatting.

## Objective

Learn whether footwear or apparel operators will engage with a specific conversation about coordinating post-purchase workflows. Matthew’s relevant contribution is consumer and lifecycle marketing judgment; Pango provides technical and commercial validation.

## Research basis

- [Pango’s YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pango) identifies Steve Rahimi as founder/CEO and includes an introduction request for fashion, beauty and home brands.
- [Pango’s product site](https://pango.ai/) describes delivery, order tracking and returns capabilities. Treat performance claims as vendor claims; do not repeat numerical improvements in outreach without approved supporting evidence.
- Ten brand-owned policy pages inform the prospect tracker. Policies reveal customer-facing rules, not internal costs, dissatisfaction, revenue, order volume or purchase intent.
- Desk research reviewed on 2026-10-29. Recheck the specific observation immediately before sending.

## Ideal customer : proposed, not confirmed

**Starting geography:** US domestic ecommerce operations, contingent on Pango’s actual carrier and integration coverage.
**Starting category:** footwear first if Steve agrees; apparel is a separate reserve cohort.
**Buyer hypothesis:** head of ecommerce or operations. Customer experience leadership may be an internal champion. Validate names and responsibility before contacting anyone.
**Positive signals:** direct online selling; size/color exchanges; more than one return pathway or policy exception; a specific handoff that can be discussed without alleging a problem.
**Commercial fit still unknown:** budget, order volume, technology, contract timing and willingness to change. These must not be scored as confirmed from a storefront.

## Prioritization

The ten accounts are a research shortlist, not a ranked list of sales-ready leads. Start validation with Thursday Boot Company, Taos and Nisolo because footwear offers a coherent first segment. Nisolo’s disclosed returns provider means incumbent fit requires an explicit check. Keep Rothy’s as reserve given its established return network. Apparel accounts demonstrate a second possible segment, not a simultaneous test.

Use three checks, each Pass / Unknown / Fail:

1. **Product fit:** Pango supports the required platform, geography and workflow.
2. **Commercial fit:** a reachable owner, a relevant priority and realistic procurement timing.
3. **Evidence quality:** a current source and a specific, accurate personalization sentence.

Only launch accounts that pass product fit and evidence quality; record commercial unknowns as discovery questions. Exclude current customers, active opportunities, do-not-contact accounts and known technical mismatches after Pango’s review.

## Positioning to test

**Core promise:** Explore whether delivery, tracking and return decisions can be coordinated more simply while preserving the brand’s customer experience.
**Angle A : coordination:** Where do exceptions require someone to move information between tools or teams?
**Angle B : exchange journey:** How are exchange eligibility, return status and next-order communication kept consistent?

These are alternative conversation openings. Do not claim higher retention, lower costs or compatibility with an incumbent unless Pango substantiates it.

## Founder intake : ready to send

Hi Steve : to keep this easy, short answers to these are enough for me to shape the pilot:

1. Which customer type and buyer role would you most like more conversations with?
2. What problem usually gets those buyers interested and which customer example can I reference?
3. What have you tried so far and which accounts should I avoid?
4. What platforms, geography or workflows would make an account a poor fit?
5. Would a four-week test with 30–40 selected brands be useful and who should receive interested replies?

## Working agreement

- Proposed Matthew time: 20 hours total over four weeks; renegotiate before adding scope.
- Founder time: asynchronous intake, one review of account criteria and copy, then one short weekly response to decisions.
- Use existing Pango-approved tools. Budget starts at \$0 incremental; any spend requires agreement.
- Steve approves product claims and representation before Matthew sends as a collaborator.
- One account/contact at a time; stop follow-ups on a reply or opt-out.
- Request portfolio permission separately, covering specific material and permitted results.
- Completion: campaign assets, activity log, outcomes with denominators and a next-step memo. No obligation to continue unpaid.
